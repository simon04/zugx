# Open static rail timetable data in Europe (GTFS / NeTEx)

Research for "several countries in one instance" (PLAN.md), checked 2026-11-15. Static timetables only, no realtime. URLs were checked by HTTP request, many feeds were downloaded and opened; anything not confirmed is marked _unverified_. Licence = the licence of the timetable data (shapes from OSM are ODbL where noted).

Legend: ✅ official, open licence, anonymous download · 🔑 official, open but registration/API key · ⚠️ official but restrictive licence · 👥 community-made only · ❌ nothing found / no railway

**Licence check (2026-11-19): no licence stated anywhere official** — under licence-first, Italy needs a written confirmation before building on it.

- **NAP (CCISS)**: the dataset pages (Trenitalia 1077621, Italo 1813935, also the English page), the asset page, the NAP info page (`/public/static/multimodal`) and the document catalogue (`/public/catalog/Document`: only NeTEx profile XSDs, guidelines, web-service specs) state no licence, terms of use, contract or attribution. The only text is the site footer "CCiSS © 2022 - All rights reserved" (the website, not a data licence). No licence field on the dataset page; the guessed JSON metadata URL `/public/api/v1/catalog/Dataset/1077621` returns 404.
- **Regione Liguria** ([ds-637](https://dati.regione.liguria.it/dataset/ds-637)): "Dati servizio pianificato TRENITALIA S.p.A.", **CC BY 4.0**, holder Regione Liguria, GTFS + NeTEx L1–L3 on `srvcarto.regione.liguria.it` — but only trains stopping in Liguria (valid 2026-06-14 – 2026-12-12). RAP Liguria feeds the NAP since 2022. The only official open licence found for Trenitalia data; it doesn't cover the national file.
- **Community labels (don't count)**: deryclem/trenitalia-gtfs puts its own conversion under CC BY 4.0, citing "Italian CAD art. 52 and EU Regulation 2017/1926", no contact with Trenitalia; over-map/velix PR #38 says the NAP datasets are "No licence – No contract" — a label not visible on the NAP pages.
- **Law (reading, not legal advice)**: CAD art. 52 ("open by default") binds public administrations; Trenitalia is a state-owned company. D.Lgs. 36/2006 as amended by D.Lgs. 200/2021 (Open Data Directive 2019/1024) covers public undertakings but excludes documents produced in providing services of general interest, which timetables arguably are. Reg. 2017/1926 requires access via the NAP, not an open licence, and allows licence agreements.
- **Next step**: ask CCISS/MIT and Trenitalia in writing whether the national NeTEx may be reused and under which licence (e.g. CC BY 4.0 as Liguria), naming the use (non-commercial site publishing derived station/train indexes).

- **France — yes, official.** `export-opendata-sncf-netex.zip` (https://eu.ftp.opendatasoft.com/sncf/plandata/export-opendata-sncf-netex.zip, 31.7 MB zip / 641 MB XML, HTTP 200, anonymous, updated 2026-11-14): French NeTEx profile (NeTEx 1.09, exported by HaCon), **59,125 ServiceJourneys** valid 2026-11-14 – 2027-04-13 (~5 months), **train numbers** as `TrainNumber` objects, **coupled train parts** as `JourneyPart`/`JourneyPartCouple`, rail submodes. Much richer than the SNCF GTFS (no shapes, no `trip_short_name`). Licence: ODbL + SNCF conditions.
- **Spain — no NeTEx found.** Renfe publishes GTFS only (data.renfe.com: AV/LD/MD and Cercanías, **CC BY 4.0** stated on the dataset pages). The national access point (nap.transportes.gob.es) accepts NeTEx per secondary sources but couldn't be read (page too large / listing 404); its Renfe GTFS mirrors need a login.
- **Italy — yes, official** (see Details: Italy).

- **Licence check (2026-11-19)**: transport.data.gouv.fr states "ODbL et Conditions Particulières d'utilisation" for `horaires-sncf`; the link goes to the platform's general ODbL page (https://doc.transport.data.gouv.fr/presentation-et-mode-demploi-du-pan/conditions-dutilisation-des-donnees/licence-odbl), not to SNCF terms. SNCF's own pages load their text by script, so a plain fetch looks empty; their content is below (checked the same day).
  - **Share-alike, as the platform reads it**: "la clause de partage à l'identique (article 4.4) concerne les informations de même nature, de même granularité, de même conditions temporelles et de même emprise géographique"; derived databases meeting that must be shared under ODbL and "publiées sur le Point d'accès national transport.data.gouv.fr, dans le format d'origine". zugx's French station/train indexes would very likely count. Not applied to OSM-derived data.
  - **Attribution**: names of the contributors and the ODbL, e.g. "Ces données sont disponibles sur transport.data.gouv.fr sous la licence « Open Database Licence » (ODbL)"; a linked "sources des données" page is enough where space is short.
- **SNCF's own terms (data.sncf.com, checked 2026-11-19)**: the publisher confirms the ODbL and adds conditions in its FAQ.
  - **Dataset record** (https://data.sncf.com/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/horaires-sncf): "HORAIRES SNCF", publisher SNCF Voyageurs, licence "Open Database License (ODbL)", linking to the CGU annex A1 (the ODbL text).
  - **CGU** (https://data.sncf.com/pages/cgu/, December 2021): art. 2, datasets may be consulted and downloaded under the ODbL; art. 8.2, SNCF grants "une licence gratuite et non-exclusive d'utilisation … selon les modalités de l'Open Database License"; art. 8.1 reserves the site itself, not the datasets. Art. 6.1: anonymous access is limited to 10,000 download requests a day, unlimited with an account. Usual liability and indemnity clauses; French law and courts.
  - **Licence FAQ** (https://data.sncf.com/pages/licence/):
    - Attribution: a "Source(s) des données" page, easy to reach from the legal notice or terms of use, naming the licensors and linking to the open-data platforms.
    - Share-alike only for derived data of the same nature, granularity, time span and area, and only when used publicly. Its examples of data to redistribute include corrected stop names, added coordinates, **corrected planned times** and added lines of the same network; nearby shops, walking routes between platforms, realtime departures or a neighbouring network's lines need not be. zugx's French station index, train index and departure plans would almost certainly count.
    - Redistribution via an easily accessible link or API, **updated immediately with every update of the original database**. SNCF updates almost daily, so this means an automated rebuild and publish (e.g. a daily CI job), or SNCF's agreement to a slower rhythm. The FAQ does not mention republishing on the PAN, which is the platform's reading.
    - No relicensing of derived data (ODbL 4.8). The app or website is a "Création produite" and keeps its own licence.
    - Commercial use is allowed (others may then redistribute the shared data freely).
    - Q11: public data is free (art. L. 324-1 CRPA), but art. L. 1115-3 code des transports (LOM) lets the licensor ask for financial compensation for passing on travel data, depending on the data concerned.
    - Questions on interpretation: open-data@sncf.fr.
  - **Mentions légales** (https://data.sncf.com/pages/mentions-legales/): site published by Société nationale SNCF; contact open-data@sncf.fr; no licence terms.
  - **Open questions for open-data@sncf.fr**: whether republishing derived data with each zugx rebuild (rather than on every SNCF update) is acceptable, and whether any L. 1115-3 compensation applies.
- Licences were not given per dataset on the listing page. It showed only counts: Licence Ouverte (11), ODbL (7), Licence mobilités (1), unspecified (1). Verify each dataset page before reuse. ODbL requires share-alike, which is a concern for derived databases.
- The SNCF national GTFS lacks shapes and `trip_short_name`, and the other French rail datasets are mostly unopened. SNCF's own NeTEx is the richer source. Regional feeds can overlap with the national one, so deduplicate TER trips.
- All the national `horaires-sncf` details were checked directly. The Transilien details come from its dataset page only. The Eurostar, Renfe, Trenitalia, Flix and regional rows come from the listing page only.

## Caveats

- **Licence ≠ access**: several clean licences need a login or API key (DELFI, MVO, Trafiklab, Danish NAP).
- **Train numbers** (`trip_short_name`) are present in ÖBB, Swiss, SNCB, gtfs-nl and Digitraffic; missing in the gtfs.de free feeds and the Entur GTFS (use NeTEx there).
- **Shapes**: ÖBB, gtfs-nl (ODbL), Luxembourg, Digitraffic, Baltic feeds have them; Swiss, SNCB, gtfs.de free don't.
- **Duplicates across borders**: neighbouring feeds contain the same cross-border trains (e.g. Srbijavoz incl. Montenegro/Hungary, Ukraine incl. Poland/Slovakia/Hungary) — dedupe by train number and day when merging.
- Community feeds: redistribution rights unclear; they depend on volunteers updating them at the timetable change.
